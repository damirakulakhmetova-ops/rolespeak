FAQs

Good questions, clear answers.

Is RoleSpeak only for nurses?

No. The broader concept includes doctors and other healthcare professionals. The team's initial live MVP focuses on nurse–patient OET Speaking role-plays; other tracks are planned.

Does RoleSpeak guarantee an OET score?

No. Any AI feedback is for educational practice and is not an official score or guarantee of exam performance.

Can I talk to an AI patient here?

This page contains an interactive text preview. Live AI voice conversation will require connecting the team's deployed MVP.

Is this affiliated with OET?

No. RoleSpeak is an independent educational concept and is not endorsed by OET.
